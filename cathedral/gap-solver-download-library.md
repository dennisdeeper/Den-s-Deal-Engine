# Cathedral — Gap Solver Download Library

Status: RESEARCHED / BUILD QUEUE — NOT PUBLISHED
Branch: cathedral/gap-solver-download-library
Date: 2026-10-03

## Cathedral decision
Gap Solver should expand from deal discovery into a practical problem-solving download library. Do not build generic high-page-count planners simply to inflate catalogue size. Every product must solve a narrow problem quickly, work on mobile where practical, and have a free/low-friction entry point feeding a more capable paid product or bundle.

## Research evidence
- Etsy UK budget planner results show 1,000+ competing products, with mature listings carrying hundreds to 10k+ reviews. Commodity budget planners are therefore validated but highly saturated.
- Etsy UK moving-planner results show 231+ relevant UK results and individual spreadsheet listings with 10k+ review/sales signals; specialist independent moving planners also sell around £12–£17.
- UK reseller spreadsheets for eBay/Vinted/Depop are currently listed around the mid-£20s and bundle inventory, fees, postage, returns, expenses and real-profit calculations.
- A home-management Etsy shop found in research reports 10,680 sales across emergency binders, maintenance planners, cleaning tools, meal planning and finance products, indicating demand beyond pure budgeting.
- Free authoritative alternatives exist for generic budgeting and moving checklists (GOV.UK/consumer guidance and Which?), so Gap Solver cannot charge merely for information that is already freely available. Paid value must come from simplification, calculations, workflow, UK-specific context, personalization, or joined-up tools.

## Red Team / Contradiction Office
1. A crowded Etsy category is evidence of transactions, not evidence that a new undifferentiated product will sell.
2. Review counts are imperfect demand proxies and may be shop-wide or accumulated over years.
3. Competitor claims about search volume or earnings are marketing claims unless independently verified; do not use them in Gap Solver sales copy.
4. Generic PDF planners are easy to copy and have severe price pressure.
5. Our advantage should be useful logic + UK specificity + cross-product intelligence, not page count or decorative design.
6. Do not copy competitor text, layout or proprietary templates. Build original structures from problem requirements and authoritative public facts.

## Product portfolio

### P0 — build first
1. **Reseller Profit Kit UK** — MONETISE
   - Buyer: eBay/Vinted/Depop/media reseller.
   - Free: one-item true-profit calculator/checklist.
   - Paid: inventory, purchase cost, sale price, platform/postage/packaging/return costs, net profit, ROI, break-even/minimum-price calculator, monthly dashboard.
   - Cathedral advantage: connect concepts from Media Engine deal verification and sourcing discipline without exposing proprietary internals.
   - Test price hypothesis: £7.99–£12.99 basic; £14.99–£19.99 enhanced bundle. Must A/B test rather than assert optimality.

2. **Moving House Control Pack UK** — MONETISE
   - Free: moving-day + who-to-notify mini checklist.
   - Paid: dated countdown, quote comparison, moving budget, room/box inventory, change-of-address tracker, meter/evidence record, essentials box, first-week checklist.
   - Test price hypothesis: £5.99–£9.99.

3. **Household Bills & Subscription Reset** — MONETISE
   - Free: 10-minute recurring-payment audit.
   - Paid: monthly/annual cost normalisation, renewal dates, cancellation/renegotiation queue, savings-before/after dashboard, irregular annual bills.
   - Test price hypothesis: £3.99–£7.99.

4. **First Home / First Flat Control Pack UK** — MONETISE
   - Combines move-in, essential purchases, utilities/admin, first-month budget, room priorities and renewal tracking.
   - Test price hypothesis: £7.99–£12.99.

5. **Emergency Home Information Pack** — VERIFY then MONETISE
   - Household contacts, utilities, insurers, pets, key documents/location references, emergency actions and home-maintenance contacts.

   - Test price hypothesis: £4.99–£8.99.

## Packaging architecture
- FREE SOLVER: one immediate useful result.
- MINI SOLVER: £1.99–£3.99 where appropriate.
- FULL SOLVER: roughly £5.99–£12.99 depending on automation/value.
- BUNDLE: related solvers bundled below sum-of-parts price.
- Avoid fake discounts, fake urgency and invented savings claims.

## Buyer journey
Problem/search landing page → free useful solver → result → relevant paid upgrade → bundle only when genuinely related → optional affiliate recommendation where approval and relevance are verified.

No generic shop wall should be the primary discovery mechanism. Catalogue navigation should be by problem: Money, Home, Moving, Selling, Family, Travel, Life Admin.

## Cathedral routing
- Chief of Staff: maintain one canonical product backlog here; kill duplicate planner workstreams.
- Portfolio Director: Gap Solver owns general-life solvers; Media Engine supplies reseller/deal intelligence; Mind Reloop remains separate wellbeing product.
- Revenue & Sales: validate free→paid funnels and bundles; affiliates are additive, not required for launch.
- Finance: model price, payment fees and support burden before launch.
- Customer Intelligence: collect search/query and funnel evidence; do not infer demand from traffic alone.
- Research: recheck competitor price/demand quarterly and before major launch.
- Product: build P0 sequentially, starting with Reseller Profit Kit UK.
- Engineering: mobile-first, zero-install where possible; spreadsheets require Excel/Google Sheets QA.
- Experience: each solver starts in <5 minutes and explains the outcome in plain English.
- QA/Release: test formulas, blank/negative/extreme inputs, mobile layout, printing, downloads, broken links and version compatibility.
- Evidence/Safety: distinguish official facts from assumptions and marketing hypotheses.
- Legal/IP/Privacy: original copy/layout; source public facts; privacy warning on sensitive household data; clear personal-use licence and digital-product terms.
- Knowledge & Memory: this file is the canonical Cathedral decision record for the library.
- Automation/Infrastructure: reuse one product manifest/catalogue and one QA checklist rather than parallel product databases.
- Resource Allocation: P0 only until conversion evidence exists; no 30-product content dump.
- Red Team: challenge demand, differentiation and price before release.
- Contradiction Office: block claims unsupported by independent evidence.

## Release gates
No live publication from this branch. A product moves to release candidate only after: original content check; formula/function QA; Excel + Google Sheets compatibility where applicable; mobile review; privacy/legal copy review; download test; product-page link test; price marked as hypothesis unless sales evidence exists; Red Team challenge closed; Den approval for live publish.
